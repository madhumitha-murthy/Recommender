# Payroll Service

## Payroll Account
- Automatic salary crediting to your bank account
- Earns bonus interest on 360 Account
- Instant salary notifications
- Linked to CPF and tax filing

## Key Benefits
- Seamless salary crediting with employer
- Bonus interest on 360 Account (salary credit category — up to 2% p.a.)
- Early salary access (selected employers)
- Simplified tax filing with auto-generated income records

## Common Objections
- "My salary goes to another bank" — Switching is simple. We provide a salary crediting form; your HR processes the change in one pay cycle.
- "I don't want all my money in one bank" — You can set up automatic transfers to other accounts after salary credit. Keep the bonus interest benefit.

## Best For
- Salaried employees not yet crediting salary here
- Customers with 360 Account (maximises bonus interest)
- New employees setting up banking for the first time

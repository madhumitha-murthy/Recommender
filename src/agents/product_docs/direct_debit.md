# Direct Debit

## GIRO / Direct Debit Service
- Automatic bill payment from your bank account
- Supports utilities, insurance premiums, loan repayments, subscriptions
- No transaction fees
- Set up online or at branch
- Age: 18 and above (with current/savings account)

## Key Benefits
- Never miss a payment — automated and on schedule
- Avoid late payment penalties on credit cards, loans, and utilities
- Contributes to positive credit history
- Earns bonus interest on 360 Account (bill payment category)

## Common Objections
- "I prefer to control when I pay" — You can set payment dates and limits. Cancel anytime online.
- "What if there's insufficient balance?" — You'll be notified before the deduction. Set up a low-balance alert.

## Best For
- Customers with multiple recurring bills
- Those who want to earn 360 Account bonus interest
- Customers with existing current account (natural add-on)

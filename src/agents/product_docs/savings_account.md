# Savings Account

## 360 Savings Account
- Earn bonus interest up to 4.05% p.a.
- Bonus categories: salary credit, card spend, insurance, investments, bill payments
- No minimum balance required
- Age: 18 and above
- Digital banking enabled

## Key Benefits
- Step-up bonus interest rewards multiple banking activities
- No lock-in period — withdraw anytime
- Linked to mobile banking app for real-time tracking
- Automatic salary crediting detection

## Common Objections
- "Interest rates are low" — The 360 Account rewards you for banking activities you're already doing. With salary credit + card spend, you can earn up to 4.05% on first S$100,000.
- "I already have a savings account elsewhere" — Consider consolidating. The bonus interest structure means your effective rate increases with each activity you bring over.

## Best For
- Salaried employees (salary credit bonus)
- Customers with existing credit card (card spend bonus)
- First-time banking customers

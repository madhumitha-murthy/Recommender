# Credit Card

## Cashback Credit Card
- Up to 5% cashback on dining, groceries, and online spend
- No annual fee for the first year
- Minimum income: S$2,500/month
- Age: 21 and above
- Contactless payment enabled

## Rewards Premium Card
- 1.4 miles per dollar on all spend
- Airport lounge access (2 complimentary visits/year)
- Minimum income: S$6,000/month
- Age: 25 and above
- Annual fee: S$192.60 (waived with S$50,000 annual spend)

## Common Objections
- "I already have a credit card from another bank" — Our cashback rates are among the highest for dining (5%) and groceries (3%). Consider using this as your primary card for daily spend.
- "I don't want annual fees" — First year is free, and we waive fees based on spend thresholds.

## Best For
- Young professionals building credit (Cashback card)
- Frequent travellers and high spenders (Premium card)
- Customers already holding a savings account (natural cross-sell)
